## What he is (as far as any archive knows)

- The **first successful adaptive combat frame** ever created by humanity,
  during the Old War. *"Not human. Not machine. Something in between."*
- It learned. It evolved. It survived.
- **All adaptive technology in existence originates from this entity** — the
  sheet's Origin box is explicit: *all Ghost Frames, Zenith Frames, XIIX
  technology, and advanced adaptive suits are derived from fragments of
  AZRAEL-0's architecture.*
- Tagline: *"He is not a weapon. He is the reason weapons were created."*

## Lore connections (verbatim from sheet)

- **TEMPLATE: Sevirum's adaptive frame** — Sevirum's unique adaptive Zenith is
  the closest living descendant of Azrael-0's architecture.
- **RELICS: linked to XIIX Project** — the hidden squad Sevirum served in is
  tied to Azrael-0 recovery/derivation.
- **TIMELINE: Pre Old War** (existence predates the war that his creation is
  attributed to — deliberate contradiction/mystery)
- **PURPOSE: Unknown**

## Canon impact — the Zenith mystery, refined (NOT resolved)

Locked canon stays locked: *no one knows how the Zeniths were made.* This
sheet adds the genealogy — Zeniths derive from **fragments** of Azrael-0 —
but relocates the mystery upward: who made Azrael-0, what he is, and what he
wants remain unknown even in-universe. The mystery box now has a face.

## Kit (boss-design goldmine)

- **Carries no weapons** — adaptive weapon manifestations generated in real
  time from battle analysis: great sword, energy spear, rail cannon, nanite
  whip, energy shield. *No two configurations are ever the same.*
- Core abilities: **Combat Evolution** (the longer the battle, the more
  dangerous he becomes), **Adaptive Reconstruction** (destruction is
  temporary), **Kinetic Absorption** (the harder you hit, the harder it hits
  back), **Prediction Engine**, **Threat Replication** (*the opponent becomes
  the blueprint* — he learns and mirrors the player's own moveset).
- **Omega Ascension** — final evolution state: transcends limitations,
  becomes "a living weapon of absolute adaptation" (massive spiked ascension
  form on sheet).
- Technical: adaptive proto-nanite frame, black titanium + gold-titanium
  composite, self-evolving power core, quantum combat analysis.
  **Weakness: none confirmed.**

## Design reading (not canon — suggestions)

- The ultimate endgame boss: an enemy that counters the player's habits via
  Threat Replication is a mechanical identity no other boss here has.
- Thematic keystone: Sevirum fighting Azrael-0 is the adaptive suit fighting
  its own ancestor — the mirror-fight the whole nanotech theme points at.
- Palette note: his black/gold sits atop the *demon* color language, not the
  human/ExZer one. If intentional, that visual rhyme is a loaded gun —
  are the demons and the First Frame connected? (Creator owns this.)
