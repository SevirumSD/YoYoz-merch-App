# Art Direction

## The system in one rule

**Dark matte base + faceted hard-surface armor + ONE signature glow color per
character, palette per faction.** Every asset obeys this; it's what makes the
whole universe read as designed rather than assembled.

## Silhouette rules (learned from the strongest sheets)

1. **One focal zone per character** — detail clusters need rest areas around
   them (Linora's visor, Conor's vertical flow, Odion's smooth planes).
2. Detail density ≠ quality. Uniform spikes everywhere = noise (Sevirum v1's
   core problem).
3. Rulers get crowns: every leader needs a distinctive head silhouette
   readable at distance (Queen, Zeakhul, Vaelthar ✓ — Zanmer ✗ pending).
4. Family/faction resemblance is free storytelling (Odion ↔ Zanmer should
   rhyme).

## Redesign briefs (creator-flagged)

### Sevirum v2 (priority — he's the main character)
- Problem: front view has no hierarchy; spikes compete everywhere.
- Keep: adaptive-modes concept, violet nanite glow, weapon integration.
- Direction: calm/smooth dormant front with ONE focal glow (chest core or
  helmet); migrate aggressive spikes to back/shoulders for side/back drama;
  +30% plain plate between detail clusters; **spikes erupt visibly when
  Assault Mode engages** — the redesign turns his flaw into his signature
  transformation.

### King Zanmer head
- Problem: smooth blank ovoid helmet on a hyper-detailed titan body; no
  royalty in the silhouette; head slightly too small.
- Keep: serene faceplate (calm of absolute confidence) as the focal point.
- Direction: crown/crest frame sweeping back, echoing the Reverse Scythe
  curve and shoulder blades; ice-blue energy channels up the neck into the
  crest; +head mass; silhouette rhyme with Odion.

## Rendering / engine targets

- Sheets are tagged "8K ULTRA RESOLUTION / UNREAL ENGINE READY" — target
  UE5, photoreal-adjacent hard surface with stylized glow VFX.
- Kortharax sheet carries full production specs (humanoid rig, PBR textures,
  LOD0-4, Niagara VFX, 120+ animation list) — use as the template for
  production briefs on all characters.
- Aria's sheet (fully rendered face, UE5 tag) sets the bar for human faces.
