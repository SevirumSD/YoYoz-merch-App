# The Faction — Canonical Lore

**Primary Antagonist Organization | Seedance 2.5**

## Overview

The Faction is a militaristic, hierarchical organization spanning multiple operational tiers and specialized unit types. Its influence permeates the world of Seedance 2.5, from covert prototype operations to mass-produced combat units to apex-tier executioners.

---

## Organizational Structure

### Tier System

1. **AGS Prototypes (Ancient Guardian Series)** — Early-generation experimental units, each one-of-a-kind or extremely limited production
2. **FCT Production Units (The Faction Combat Tier)** — Mass-produced soldiers with standardized loadouts and behavioral profiles
3. **Omega Tier** — Enhanced special operations units with expanded capabilities
4. **Omega-Prime / Executioners** — Apex-tier units designed for singular, strategic objectives
