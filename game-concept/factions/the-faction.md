Identity, role, and origin: unknown (open question).

## The false-flag doctrine (creator-confirmed)

The FCT units are **starter/early-game enemies manufactured to scare the
public**: The Faction deploys them as unknown horrors, then publicly
"defeats" them — casting itself as **Earth's defense**. The monsters and
the heroes have the same manufacturer. Design consequence: the units are
deliberately styled as *nightmares*, not human tech — bone-white plating,
reaper hoods, horns — costumed to read as demons/aliens to a terrified
public (and to the player, first time through).

## The pilots are prisoners

The single darkest detail, consistent across sheets: pilot interface =
**Neural Link (Forced Sync)**. Warden: "Locked neural link with **zero user
freedom**… all critical functions are controlled by The Faction." Wraith:
"obedience conditioning… emotional dampeners reduce hesitation and increase
operational loyalty." Passives include **Total Obedience** ("cannot disobey
direct Faction commands") and **Pain Suppression** ("ensures continued
operation"). The Faction's enforcement units are crewed by people who
cannot say no. Rescue-the-pilot mission material; also the thematic inverse
of the Ghosts (Zeniths amplify their wearers' will — FCT frames erase it).

## Confirmed canon (from creator)

## Confirmed canon (from creator)

- The FCT enforcement line exists as **false-flag starter enemies**: deployed
  to terrorize the public, then destroyed on camera so The Faction looks
  like Earth's defense.
- **The Vanguard/Ascendant frame programs (Stray, Sigma Knight, Seraph) were
  orchestrated by The Faction.**
- Public purpose: defend humanity against new, unknown enemies.
- **True purpose: eliminate the Zeniths — which The Faction considers the
  biggest threat in existence.**
- Human-piloted frames, sold to the public as hope. The heroic lore text on
  the Vanguard sheets ("no one was coming to save them anymore," the
  Founding Charter) is therefore **in-universe narrative shaped by The
  Faction** — propaganda that may be sincere at the soldier level and
  cynical at the top.

## What this implies (unconfirmed — creator owns)

- The "disappearance of the Ghosts" and the "decline of the Zeniths" in the
  public record may be *outcomes The Faction engineered*, not background
  events. History written by the victor-orchestrator.
- Anti-Apostle weapon ratings on Vanguard frames double as anti-Zenith
  capability — adaptive-tech killers work on both.
- Relationship to Aegis Syndicate, Trident Industries, XIIX, and the Old War
  Black Archive: unknown. Any of them could be fronts, hosts, or enemies.
- Seraph's "technologies ancient even the Apostles could not master" —
  if The Faction sourced them, The Faction may hold Azrael-0 fragments or
  worse.

## Design reading

The Faction gives the story its human antagonist and its central irony: the
Ghosts saved humanity, and humanity's own shadow government built angels to
erase them for it. Every faction fears the Zeniths; only The Faction *did
something about it.*
