# The Faction — The Hidden Orchestrator

> The last name from the original concept pitch, and the only power with no
> sheet — **by design**. The Faction doesn't field armies under its own flag;
> it authors programs, writes cover stories, and lets humanity build its
> weapons for it. No dossier exists because the Curators Archive hasn't
> caught them yet. *(Creator-confirmed canon, sheets pending.)*

## Confirmed canon (from creator)

- **The Vanguard/Ascendant frame programs (Stray, Sigma Knight, Seraph) were
  orchestrated by The Faction.**
- Public purpose: defend humanity against new, unknown enemies.
- **True purpose: eliminate the Zeniths — which The Faction considers the
  biggest threat in existence.**
- Human-piloted frames, sold to the public as hope. The heroic lore text on
  the Vanguard sheets ("no one was coming to save them anymore," the
  Founding Charter) is therefore **in-universe narrative shaped by The
  Faction** — propaganda that may be sincere at the soldier level and
  cynical at the top.

## What this implies (unconfirmed — creator owns)

- The "disappearance of the Ghosts" and the "decline of the Zeniths" in the
  public record may be *outcomes The Faction engineered*, not background
  events. History written by the victor-orchestrator.
- Anti-Apostle weapon ratings on Vanguard frames double as anti-Zenith
  capability — adaptive-tech killers work on both.
- Relationship to Aegis Syndicate, Trident Industries, XIIX, and the Old War
  Black Archive: unknown. Any of them could be fronts, hosts, or enemies.
- Seraph's "technologies ancient even the Apostles could not master" —
  if The Faction sourced them, The Faction may hold Azrael-0 fragments or
  worse.

## Design reading

The Faction gives the story its human antagonist and its central irony: the
Ghosts saved humanity, and humanity's own shadow government built angels to
erase them for it. Every faction fears the Zeniths; only The Faction *did
something about it.*
