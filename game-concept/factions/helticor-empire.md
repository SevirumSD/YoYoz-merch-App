# The Helticor Empire

> A galactic nonhuman civilization — **separate from the demon powers** — with
> a royal house, unified clans, and its own homeworld (**Helticor Prime**).
> Trident Industries built entire war-machine lines just to hunt them
> ("Anti-Helticor" is a design requirement on ExZer-01).
>
> Art language: **polished silver + ice blue energy** — the cold mirror of the
> demons' black-and-gold.

## King Zanmer — The Crown of Helticor
- High King of the Helticors, Sovereign of War, Master Strategist.
- Class: Royal Titan / Commander. 7'7". Faction: Helticors • Royal House.
- **Threat level: Omega+ • EXISTENTIAL** — the only entity carrying the
  "Existential" flag so far.
- Unified the fractured clans through strategy, strength, and "a vision beyond
  mortal comprehension." *"Many consider him not only a king, but a living war
  doctrine."*
- **Father of High General Odion** (Ghost roster — see `ghosts.md`).

### Kit
- **Helticor Energy Axes ×2** → retractable **Spike Connector** joins both
  axes into **Reverse Scythe Mode** (area slashing, disrupts energy/shields/
  formations, cleaves titans). Signature transforming weapon system.
- Shield gauntlet; adaptive armor forged from Helticor Prime materials that
  "grows stronger with its king."
- Psychic powers (hidden): mental dominion, psionic blast, thought
  manipulation, battle precognition, psychic barrier.
- Elemental affinity: energy, psionic, gravity, lightning, void — absorbs and
  redirects most energies.
- *"A king does not follow the future. He shapes it."* / "No army has ever
  defeated him. No strategist has outthought him. No warrior has outmatched him."

## Odion — the heir who left
- "Daughter of the Helticor King," 7 ft, Helticor royal bloodline, engineered
  from the purest bloodline + most advanced nanotechnology.
- Fights alongside the human Ghost squad. **Why the heir of Helticor serves on
  a human deniable-ops team is unrevealed canon** — the single biggest untold
  story in the setting. Creator owns this reveal.
- Full sheet + kit: see `ghosts.md` and `../reference-sheets/ghosts/odion.png`.
