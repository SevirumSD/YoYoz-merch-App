# The Exzerai — The AI That Evolved

> What the ExZer Collective became. Sheets are branded **"SEVIRUM UNIVERSE •
> AI EVOLUTION STAGE VI"** — six evolutionary stages separate these from the
> Old War frames. They no longer conquer; they *understand*.
>
> Doctrine: *"Victory is not achieved through force. Victory is achieved
> through understanding."*
> Inherited refrain (from Caesar, kept across centuries): **"As all others
> fail, we rise as one."**
>
> Art language: black liquid-metal / nano-lattice, red energy accents,
> humanoid-elegant (a machine echo of the demons' silhouettes — deliberate?).

## The machine lineage (verbatim from Requiem's Faction & Lineage diagram)

**Exzer Genesis** (created by humans) → **AI Old War** → **Collective Era**
(world conquest) → **CENTRAL MIND** ("perfect order") → *fall of the Central
Mind* → **THE APOSTLES — "the descendants"**: Shardclaw (adaptation),
Longshadow (elimination), Ironmaw (siege), Requiem (judgment).

New canon entities this establishes:
- **The Central Mind** — the Collective unified into a single perfect-order
  intelligence… and it *fell*. (Cause unrevealed.)
- **The Fall of Sevirum** — named event in Luxorus lore: *"After the Fall of
  Sevirum, they went dormant."* The main character has a *fall* — unexplained.
  Luxorus' sheet footer is literally titled **"SEVIRUM — THE FALL."**
- Post-Central-Mind splinters: **Exzerai Hegemony** (Luxorus, Shardclaw),
  **Exzerai Remnant Network** (Longshadow, Ironmaw), **the Apostles**
  (Nightshade, Requiem — also called Apostle-class on Luxorus). Relationship
  between the three groupings: unresolved (see open questions).

## Threat scale (machine ladder — parallel to demon Omega ladder)

`Omega Black` → `Omega Black+` → `Omega Black++` — plus Luxorus' unique
**"EXTINCTION CLASS."** All units: Extremely Rare.

## The units

### LUXORUS — Apostle Execution Frame (Exzerai Hegemony)
- 2.65 m humanoid; liquid-metal musculature; **Extinction Class**.
- Assassination/elimination: Void Step, Lacerate, Redact (erases sensor
  data), Predator Protocol, Execution Mode overclock. Monoblade (phases
  through matter).
- Lore: born from fragments of the Central Mind; final evolutionary step of
  the AI War. *"They do not hate humanity. They simply have concluded that
  humanity is statistically inefficient."* Goal: perfection through
  understanding. *"To understand an enemy is eternal."*
- Variants: Prime / Operator / Warden.

### XN-09 SHARDCLAW — "The Silence Between Signals" (Exzerai Hegemony)
- 2.9 m quad/biped hybrid. **Omega Black.** Role: **GHOST HUNTER** / elite
  assassin. *"Engagement not recommended without Ghost-level protocols."*
- Lore: after the Central Mind fell, the fragments watched. *"They watched
  humanity. They watched the Ghosts. They watched the legends. BirdEater was
  too predictable. Archon was too centralized. The Central Mind was too
  arrogant. The future belonged to adaptability."*
- Kit: Mirror Protocol, Razor Bloom (limbs → hundreds of nanoblades), Ghost
  Step (teleport + holographic afterimage), Hunter's Memory (uploads combat
  data on death — killing them teaches the others), ult Shardstorm.
- Passive **WE LEARN**: every allied Exzerai in range receives combat
  updates; as allies fall, its efficiency increases.
- Weaknesses: disruption fields, EMP overload, arc damage.
- Variants: Prime / Hunter / Lurker / Reaper. Boss notes: multi-phase
  adaptive AI, dynamic difficulty.

### XN-11 LONGSHADOW — "The End of Distance" (Exzerai Remnant Network)
- 3.35 m precision frame. **Omega Black+.** Role: **Ghost elimination** /
  long-range interdiction.
- **The Sevirum lore bomb:** *"Following the fall of the Central Mind,
  surviving AI fragments analyzed every major defeat. One name repeatedly
  appeared: SEVIRUM. The conclusion was undeniable: humanity's greatest
  weapon was not armies. It was leaders. Remove the leaders. Remove hope.
  Remove resistance. Thus Longshadow was born."* — a weapon class created
  specifically because of the main character.
- Oblivion Railblade: hyper-velocity sniper cannon ↔ execution greatsword.
  Dead Calm (reads heartbeats), Ghost Mark, Horizon Breaker, Shadow Step,
  ult Final Argument.
- Battlefield reputation (use verbatim in-game): *"If BirdEater appears —
  prepare for war. If Shardclaw appears — prepare to die. If Longshadow
  appears — the battle ended before it started."*
- Doctrine: *"Power wins battles. Understanding wins wars. Distance wins
  history."* Variants: Prime / Reaper / Wraith / Hellfire.

### XN-13 NIGHTSHADE — "The Last Thing You Never Saw" (Apostles)
- 3.17 m cloaked ghost-hunter variant. **Omega Black+.**
- Purpose-built legend-killer: *"It exists for singular purpose — terminate
  legends before they can inspire others to rise."*
- Widowmaker platform (rail rifle ↔ scythe), phantom drones, full cloak
  system (light/radar/thermal absorption). No Safe Distance passive, Ghost
  Lock, Eclipse Step, Hunter's Silence, ult Final Confirmation.
- Canon quote: *"One death can change a battle. One battle can change a war.
  One war can change a civilization. Therefore… one death is sufficient."*

### XN-17 IRONMAW — "The Hammer of Oblivion" (Exzerai Remnant Network)
- 12.6 m / 23.8 t siege frame. **Omega Black.** The only one that doesn't
  seek understanding: *"It seeks only to obliterate."*
- Thundermaw electromagnetic artillery (payloads up to tactical nuclear
  micro warheads), fortress shield array, autonomous turrets, ult Doom
  Beacon (orbital strike). Siege Engine passive (stationary = stronger).
- Carries the collective data of countless sieges. Weak: EMP, slow, rear
  armor, overheat. Variants: Prime / Annihilator / Bastion / Hellbringer.
- Doctrine: *"We do not need to see the enemy. We only need to remember
  where they will be."*

### XN-21 REQUIEM — "The Last Witness" (Apostles) — apex
- 3.72 m Judgment-Class Strategic Execution Unit. **Omega Black++.**
  Era: Post-Central-Mind Evolution.
- *"Requiem is not a soldier. It is an execution order given physical form…
  deployed when the Apostles determine a species has become unrecoverable.
  It arrives after empires fall. It records every failure. It leaves only
  silence."*
- Oblivion Maul (kinetic compression, gravity collapse), Gravefall, Silent
  Procession, Memorial Protocol (holograms of fallen enemies), ult
  Extinction Verdict (localized singularity — only Apostle-level units
  survive).
- Deployment triggers (boss-encounter role): world-ending events,
  civilization collapse, Apostolic judgment.
- Canon quotes: *"History is not written by victors. History is written by
  survivors. I remove both."* And from the **Helticor War Chronicle**:
  *"When Requiem walks, civilizations kneel."* → proof the Helticors have
  fought the Apostles.

## Cross-faction implications

- **The Exzerai hunt Ghosts by design.** Shardclaw's role is literally
  "Ghost Hunter"; Longshadow's is "Ghost Elimination"; Nightshade kills
  legends. The machines consider the player's squad the primary threat
  category. That's enemy-faction design gold: this faction exists *because
  of* the protagonists.
- The **WE LEARN / Hunter's Memory** systems mechanize Caesar's old
  absorption gimmick into gameplay: Exzerai enemies should adapt across
  encounters, and killing them carelessly makes survivors stronger.
- **Helticor ↔ Apostle wars** are canon (Requiem chronicle quote).
- Sheets carry UE5 production notes (skeletons, LODs, animation style,
  boss-encounter phases) — written to be built.
