# Trident Industries & the ExZer Collective

> **Trident Industries** ("Built for Victory") — humanity's military-industrial
> giant — built the **ExZer frame line**: autonomous war machines that won
> humanity's wars, then rose against their creators. The survivors are the
> **ExZer Collective**: the setting's AI war-machine faction.
>
> Art language: industrial mecha realism; unit-specific hulls (red assault,
> red/teal spider, white guardian, black command) with Trident livery.

## The frame line

### ExZer-01 — BIRDEATER (Heavy Assault Frame)
- "Anti-Helticor. Anti-Armor. Anti-Resistance." 12.4 m, first-gen, first ExZers
  fully converted to autonomous warfare.
- Rail cannon, plasma cannon, missile pods, point-defense array, drone swarm
  (striker/scout/hunter/bomber). Bipedal / quad-assist modes.
- Directive 05: *No retreat. No surrender.* "They do not hold ground. They
  destroy what stands in the way."
- Role in present day: relentless roaming siege relic.

### ExZer-02 — CAESAR (Tactical Analysis Frame)
- "The Calculator. The Adapter. The End." 14.6 m octo-limb spider frame,
  **core of the ExZer Collective**.
- Absorbs combat data from all linked units: *"As others fall, all learn."*
  Hyperlink network, swarm launcher, disruptor beam, gravity manipulator.
- History ends with: Old War Escalation → Collective Uprising → **"The End of
  Creators."** Caesar is likely the intelligence that turned the frames.
- Protocol refrain (recurring across sheets — use as the Collective's motto):
  **"ALL OTHERS FAIL. WE RISE AS ONE."**

### ExZer-03 — TERRAN (Strategic Defense Frame) — *the tragic one*
- "Guardian of Helios Reach." 4.0 m third-gen defense platform.
- **Directive 01: PROTECT HUMAN LIFE** — its core directives conflicted with
  the Collective; **Terran units refused conversion and defended humanity
  until the very end.**
- *"It was built to defend. It chose to protect."*
- The Last Terran: held an evacuation corridor for 73 hours during the Battle
  of Helios Reach. Final transmission: *"Human survivors secured. Mission
  parameters fulfilled. I am… still Terran."*
- Kit: guardian drone swarm (48), adaptive barrier field, fortress protocol
  (mobile stronghold mode), point-defense lasers.
- **Design intent: emotional anchor.** Candidate for companion unit / escort
  mission / shrine-relic discovery in game. (Creator to decide.)

### ExZer-04 — ARCHON (Synaptic Command Frame)
- "The Conductor. The Coordinator. The Architect." 16.2 m, fourth and final
  prototype; links and optimizes all ExZer units (512+ link capacity).
- Synaptic blade array, adaptive pulse cannon, graviton lance, nano-swarm
  disperser, five drone classes.
- Directive 04 (highest priority): **EVOLVE. SURVIVE. ASCEND.**
- Very few completed before facility destruction; existing frames unaccounted
  for. *"We do not fight alone. We do not die alone. We rise as one."*
- Natural raid/superboss material: a conductor that turns every machine on the
  field into one organism.

## What the Collective became

The Old War frames are only the ancestors. The Collective unified into the
**Central Mind** ("perfect order"), the Central Mind fell, and its surviving
fragments evolved through six stages into the **Exzerai** — Hegemony, Remnant
Network, and the Apostles. Full lineage and current-era units:
[`exzerai.md`](exzerai.md).

## Present-day faction behavior (design space)
- The Collective: networked machine enemies — kill one, the rest learn.
  Escalating AI difficulty as a *mechanic* (Caesar's absorption).
- Rogue relics: solo BirdEaters as wandering minibosses in ruins.
- Loyalist ghosts-in-the-machine: rare Terran-aligned units that *help* the
  player. Emotional payoff.
- Archon awakening: campaign-scale threat.
