# The Vanguard Initiative — Humanity's Answer

> **Human Vanguard Coalition** → **Human Ascendant Coalition.** Piloted
> frames built by ordinary humanity for the eras *after* the legends:
> the sheets are stamped **"Human Ascension Era"** and **"Ascendant Era."**
>
> The founding premise is the biggest timeline reveal in canon:
> *"After the **disappearance of the Ghosts** and the **decline of the
> Zeniths**, humanity faced a terrifying reality: no one was coming to save
> them anymore."*
>
> Founding Charter (use verbatim): *"The Ghosts bought us time. The Zeniths
> showed us what was possible. **Now it is our turn.**"*
>
> Art language: battle-worn white/ceramic + warning markings (Stray),
> gunmetal + sentinel gold (Sigma Knight), radiant white-gold halos (Seraph
> — humanity literally building angels to answer the demons).

## Human threat ladder (third parallel scale)

`OMEGA HUMAN` (Stray) → `OMEGA HUMAN+` (Sigma Knight) → `OMEGA+ (Existential)`
(Seraph — a *human* frame reaching King Zanmer's tier).

## The frames

### VF-86 STRAY — "The First Vanguard" (Mark I, Human Ascension Era)
- 2.56 m piloted adaptive combat frame (human neural interface — humans back
  in the cockpit after centuries of autonomous machines). Status: **Active
  Production Template** — mass-produced hope.
- Born from recovered ExZer data, **Ghost combat records**, Apostle
  battlefield encounters, and **Zenith battle archives** — humanity
  reverse-engineering its own legends.
- Identity line: *"Not a god. Not a legend. A soldier."* Design philosophy:
  where Apostles pursue perfection, Stray pursues *persistence* — adapt,
  endure, protect, survive.
- M-12 Breacher cannon (anti-Apostle suppression), variable utility tail,
  Last Directive passive (stronger when civilians threatened), ult
  **Humanity Stands** (safety limits removed when protected personnel are
  in danger).
- Deployed as line holders, evacuation defenders, civilian protection:
  *"They are not built to win wars. They are built to ensure humanity lives
  to fight another day."*

### SIGMA KNIGHT (T) — Mark III Spec. Rig (Sigma Program)
- 2.7 m Titan Vanguard Frame, **Omega Human+**, elite deployment.
  Titan Form (siege) ↔ Knight Form (ascended) transformation.
- Built from centuries of warfare data and **recovered Terran-class
  ExZerSuit technology** — the loyal machine's legacy lives on in
  humanity's defenders. *"They are not just machines. They are the
  embodiment of humanity's will to stand, fight, and take back the future."*
- Bastion Blade, Vanguard tail assembly, Sigma Core. Passive **Forward
  Only** (retreat penalties — doctrine as mechanics), Breach Formation,
  Iron March, Guardian Line, ult Last Charge.
- Motto: *"We do not retreat. We advance. We prevail."*

### SX-01 SERAPH — Ascendant Frame (Mark I, Ascendant Era)
- 2.38 m (3.12 m Guardian Form) — **Human Ascendant Coalition**, threat
  **OMEGA+ (Existential)**. Zero-point singularity core; environment rating
  includes *Space / Dimensional*.
- Lore bomb: *"After the **fall of the Zeniths**, humanity uncovered **the
  truth behind the ExZerSuit-Frames** and **the coming war that would dwarf
  all previous threats**. The Ascendant Initiative was born to create frames
  capable of facing the unknowable… using **technologies ancient even the
  Apostles could not master**. It is not a weapon. It is humanity's next
  evolution."*
- Solar Lance (dimensional pierce, **anti-Apostle / anti-Titan**), Lumen
  Orbitals, Horizon Step, Radiant Barrier, Halo Swarm, ult Ascension
  Protocol. Dual mode: Guardian (titan) ↔ Ascendant (humanoid).
- Motto: *"We do not fight to survive anymore. We ascend to secure the
  future."*
- Visual thesis: white-gold radiance and halos — the deliberate counter-image
  to the demons' black-gold. Humanity's angels.

## What this faction does to the timeline

Era chain now readable across all sheets:
**Old War** (ExZer uprising) → **Collective Era → Central Mind → its fall** →
**Ghosts / Sevirum era** (the playable "now"?) → **Fall of Sevirum** →
**disappearance of the Ghosts + decline/fall of the Zeniths** → **Human
Ascension Era** (Vanguard, Stray) → **Ascendant Era** (Seraph, "the coming
war"). The Ghosts become the *mythology* of later humanity — the player's
squad is the legend future soldiers are built from.
