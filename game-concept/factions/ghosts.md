# The Ghosts — Player Squad

> A deniable, independent operative team. The world's factions can't control,
> recruit, or kill them — officially, they don't exist. Every member wears a
> **Zenith**-class nanosuit. All sheet affiliations read **INDEPENDENT** —
> that's the point, not a gap.

## The Zenith suits

- Apex nanotechnology; name means "highest point."
- **Origin: unknown — canonically, no one knows how the Zeniths were made,
  including the wearers.** (Creator decision, deliberate. Do not resolve.)
- **Sevirum's Zenith is the only fully *adaptive* suit** — unique even among
  Zeniths.
- Demon intelligence rates "high-rank Ghost nanotech suits" as the benchmark
  for regeneration/speed comparisons (see Belthizar dossier).

## Character notes

### Sevirum — the main character
- Only adaptive Zenith. Suit continuously evolves in real time (assault /
  defense / stealth / mobility modes, nanite repair sequence).
- **Hidden history: served in a classified squad designated "XIIX."**
  Connection suspected to Aria / Gustave / Conor shared past (see
  `aegis-syndicate.md`).
- **Defeated the demon tactician Belthizar** in a critical operation —
  Belthizar was exiled for that failure and has returned obsessed with
  "breaking" him. Sevirum is the personal-rival anchor of the demon arc.
- Aria's biography lists "**Gustave Sevirum** and Conor" as the only constants
  of her hidden past — surname collision with Sevirum is *unresolved canon*
  (see open questions).
- **Sheet status: v1, creator-flagged for full redesign** (front-facing
  especially). See `art-direction.md` for redesign brief.

### Odion — the defector royal
- Father: **King Zanmer**, Crown of Helticor. Her lineage box on his sheet
  lists her as "7'1" General."
- Why the heir of Helticor fights beside human Ghosts is **the biggest untold
  story in canon** — creator owns this reveal.
- Kit: hyper sense (near-future sight), adaptive fighting AI, all-weapon
  mastery, royal presence.

### The dossier voice
Ghost/Zenith Intelligence compiles enemy dossiers via the **Curators Archive**
(see demon sheets: "BY: CURATORS ARCHIVE — FOR: GHOSTS / ZENITH INTELLIGENCE",
clearance Omega). Unknown enemies are literally titled "UNKNOWN" until
identified — lore delivery is gameplay.
