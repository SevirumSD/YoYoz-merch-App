# The Demon Powers — Bestiary & Politics

> Ancient nonhuman civilization(s) predating "the first sky." Not a monster
> horde: a political ecosystem of rival orders with hierarchy, exile, and
> ambition. All entries below are **in-universe intelligence dossiers**
> (Curators Archive → Ghosts / Zenith Intelligence). "UNKNOWN" names mean the
> Ghosts haven't identified the subject yet.
>
> Art language: **matte black + gold**, living armor, hidden true forms.
> Threat ladder: Omega → Omega+ → Omega++ → Omega+++.

## Factions at a glance

| Faction | Known members | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **The Covenants** | The Unknown Queen (ruler), Xaryth (supreme defender), Vaelthar (high general) | The ruling order. Lawful, hierarchical, ancient. |
| **The Abyssals** | Zeakhul (winged host commander), Malthyros (the power beneath the power), Belthizar (exiled) | Chaotic parallel host; scheming and psionic. |
| **The Shattered Choir** | Kortharax (leader) | Void-corruption cult; wants reality fractured. |
| **The Demon Legion** | Vorathen (second-in-command, loyal to Xaryth*) | Defensive military order. |

Xaryth pending creator confirmation (see open questions).

## The Covenants

### UNKNOWN — Queen of the Covenants ("Top Queen")
- **Threat: OMEGA+++ (unique).** Class: Ancient Manipulator Demon. 8'2".
- Supreme strategist; orchestrates rather than fights: *"I do not conquer. I
  do not destroy. I orchestrate. The Covenants exist because I allow it. And
  so do you."*
- Abilities: supreme intellect, shadow orchestration, limited reality
  manipulation, legion command (voiceless), forbidden ancient knowledge.
- Hidden **Ancient Way form** — 5-stage transformation ending in a
  dragon-scale entity; revealed once in all history.
- Relationships: values Xaryth's defense, aims Zeakhul like a spear, intertwines
  plans with Malthyros, keeps Belthizar checked, treats Vaelthar as a weapon.
- Locations: Obsidian Throne, Infinite Spire, Shattered Library, Veil Between
  Stars, the Ancient Way (hidden realm).
- **Endgame antagonist by construction.**

### Xaryth — The Unshakable
- Highest commander of Covenant defensive forces. Omega++. 7'7". Born from the
  First Pact.
- Living-stone armor grown from abyssal bone; **spine sword / shield system**
  morphs from his vertebrae — weapons are his body.
- Wall of Oblivion (impenetrable barriers), Defensive Shaper (terrain →
  fortress), immune to fear/manipulation.
- Hidden transformation: winged true form — "the sky trembles… even demons bow."
- *"A wall does not run. A wall does not falter. A wall does not break."*

### Vaelthar — The Ascended Warlord
- High General of the Covenants, one of the **Ascended Few**. Omega++ /
  Legendary. 8'4". **Brother of Belthizar** — considers him a disgrace.
- Living armor blades, Covenant war spear, Bloodrage greatblade, hellfire
  chainglaive. Bloodlust aura (strikes heal him), Apocalyptic Descent AoE.
- Daemon hierarchy on his sheet: Ascended Few → High General → Warlords →
  Commanders → Legionnaires → Abyssal Spawn.
- Weaknesses: pride, emotional provocation (mentions of Belthizar), overextension.
- *"We are the storm. We are the end. You are the blood that waters our victory."*

## The Abyssals

### Zeakhul — The Sky's Dread
- Commander of the Winged Host. Omega++. 7'7" (true form far larger).
  Ancient manipulator; predates much of the Abyssal legions.
- Dual ancient bat'leths of abyssal meteorite + living bone; living armor that
  manifests wings; Word Weaver (reshapes outcomes, never lies).
- 4-stage transformation; **Last Resort form** darkens the heavens.
- Rarely fights directly — weakens minds first. Vulnerable if caught
  pre-transformation.
- Rivalry: silent, deep rivalry with Vaelthar. Feared by the Abyssals as an omen.
- *"I do not rule through fear. I ascend above it. When I rise, even the skies learn to kneel."*

### Malthyros — The Word Twister
- "The power beneath the power." Omega, status **secretly supreme**. 7'7".
  Born from **the Underlying** — the hidden layer beneath reality.
- Does not lie; bends truth: *"I do not lie. I simply show you the path that
  leads where I desire."*
- Bone axes forged from titan bones, embedded in armor when idle. Words of
  Influence, Thread Weaving, Fate Bender, War Architect.
- Hidden form = the Underlying's voice; using it strains his existence.
- Quote defining the demon triangle: *"Belth adapts. Vael conquers. But I
  decide what they adapt to, and what they conquer."*

### UNKNOWN — The Word Twister (second dossier)
- Same title, same bone-axe weapon system, faction Abyssals, **Omega+**,
  unknown agenda; "his true name is forbidden."
- **Open question:** same entity as Malthyros at different intel levels, a
  predecessor, or an imposter. Creator to rule. Until then: keep both dossiers.

### Belthizar — The Calculated Ruin
- Elite demon strategist/duelist. **Omega-class.** 7'2". Faction listed as
  "The Abyssal Host" — **exiled**. Status: thought dead — ACTIVE.
- **Personal history with Sevirum:** led a critical operation against him;
  a single miscalculation caused his defeat, disgrace, exile. Returned
  wiser, deadlier: *"He will not just kill Sevirum. He will break him."*
- *"You didn't kill me, Sevirum. You made me something better."*
- Kit: Abyssal Analysis (prediction), Shadow Step, Calculated Strike, Dread
  Regeneration. Speed equalized to Ghost-class combatants; healing rate
  comparable to high-rank Ghost nanotech suits.
- **In-game role (explicit on sheet): Elite Boss / recurring Rival.**
  Weaknesses: overconfidence, the loss to Sevirum haunts him, disruption slows
  his regen.
- Brother: Vaelthar (mutual contempt).

## The Shattered Choir

### Kortharax — The Voidreaver
- The Abyssal Sovereign, Crown of Ruin, **Echo of the First Fracture**. Omega.
  2.40 m. Former high seraph of the Celestial Host who chose the Void during
  the First Fracture; the Void consumed and reforged him into living armor.
- Leads the Shattered Choir; goal: fracture the material plane so the Void can
  feast on all existence. Every claimed soul strengthens his armor-body.
- Kit: Abyssal Step, Crown of Thorns, Soul Harvest, Void Dominion; ultimate
  **The First Fracture** (armor shatters → celestial-void entity; kills spawn
  void clones). Raid form: **Avatar of Ruin**.
- Weak to holy/celestial damage; slower than agile classes; baitable.
- Sheet includes full Unreal production specs (rigging, LODs, Niagara VFX,
  animation set) — most production-ready demon.
- *"Reality is but a thin veil. I am the hand that tears it away."*

## The Demon Legion

### Vorathen — The Bastion Warden
- Second-in-command of the Demon Army; Commander of the Defensive Legion.
  Threat: Extreme. 7'0". Loyal to Xaryth — and locked in constant professional
  rivalry with him over defensive philosophy.
- Twin bone shields (merge into one massive shield / thrown), bone greatsword
  that splits into dual blades; life-force siphon on every strike.
- Flanked always by **the Twins of Vorathen** (guards Vexor & Maldor) — a
  moving fortress triad.
- Ultimate: **Fortress of Ruin** (shield slam → bone/dark-energy shockwave,
  massive defensive boost).
- *"We do not fall. We endure. And in endurance, we destroy."*

## Unaligned

### Zephyrith — The Unbound Edge / The Unsworn Resolve *(two sheet versions)*
- Higher Arch Demon, independent contractor. **Omega++** (v2) / Omega+ (v1).
  6'7". Faction: NONE. Cannot be summoned, bound, or owned.
- Accepts only **impossible tasks**. *"You set the impossible — I make it
  inevitable."* If he accepts, you have his word; if he refuses, you never see
  him again.
- Bone katana (sharper than obsidian) + rare bone greataxe (cleaves mountains).
  Untraceable presence; hidden **Abyssal Shift** form distorts reality.
- Even the Covenants can only set a price. *"He is the edge between failure
  and inevitability."*
- **Canon note:** v2 "Unbound Edge" treated as current unless creator says
  otherwise; v1 "Unsworn Resolve" kept for ability names (Shadow Rush, Bone
  Veil, Serrated Arcs, Siphon Strike, Dusk Ascension).

## Relationship web (from cross-referenced dossiers)

- **Queen** orchestrates all; only she outranks the field commanders.
- **Xaryth ↔ Vorathen:** master/loyalist + rival perfectionists of defense.
- **Vaelthar ↔ Belthizar:** brothers; exalted conqueror vs exiled calculator.
- **Vaelthar ↔ Zeakhul:** silent deep rivalry (conquest vs manipulation).
- **Malthyros ↔ everyone:** the puppeteer nobody can fully escape.
- **Zephyrith ↔ everyone:** the mercenary nobody can own.
- **Belthizar → Sevirum:** the personal war that bridges demons and Ghosts.
