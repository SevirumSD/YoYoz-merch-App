## Vision one-liner

A squad of deniable operatives in apex-technology nanosuits — **the Ghosts, wearing
Zeniths** — fight across a war between humanity's remnants, a rogue machine
collective, the Helticor Empire, and the demon Covenants. Fast, stylish
hack-and-slash action (*Implosion: Never Lose Hope* DNA) built in **Unreal Engine 5**.

## Design pillars

1. **Game-feel first** — melee combat that feels incredible before anything else
   (hitstop, hit VFX, dodge i-frames, combo flow).
2. **One art language** — dark matte materials, faceted armor, a single glowing
   accent color per character/faction. Readable silhouettes.
3. **Intel as storytelling** — lore is delivered as in-universe dossiers
   (Curators Archive / Zenith Intelligence). What the player knows = what the
   Ghosts have learned.
4. **Mystery is load-bearing** — the origin of the Zenith suits is unknown *to
   everyone in-universe, including the wearers*. Never resolve it casually.

## The world in one paragraph

Roughly four centuries after the **Old War** — when Trident Industries' autonomous
ExZer war frames rose against their creators ("the End of Creators") — humanity
shares a scarred galaxy with the surviving **ExZer Collective**, the
**Helticor Empire** of King Zanmer, and the ancient demon orders (**the
Covenants**, **the Abyssals**, **the Shattered Choir**, **the Demon Legion**)
whose politics predate the first sky. Among them move the **Ghosts**: a
deniable, independent squad wearing **Zenith** nanosuits of unknown origin —
including **Sevirum**, bearer of the only *adaptive* Zenith, veteran of a hidden
squad designated **XIIX**, and the man who broke the demon Belthizar. And beneath
all of it, older than the war itself, the prototype every adaptive technology
descends from is still active: **AZRAEL-0, the First Frame.**

## Threat-level ladder (canon)

`Omega` → `Omega+` → `Omega++` → `Omega+++` (Queen of the Covenants — unique)
— plus **"Existential"** flag (King Zanmer), and above the entire ladder:
**OMEGA-PRIME** (AZRAEL-0 — "unknown superior entity," threat to all factions).
Machines run a parallel ladder: `Omega Black` → `Omega Black+` →
`Omega Black++` (Requiem), plus Luxorus' unique **Extinction Class**.
Later human frames run a third: `Omega Human` → `Omega Human+` →
`Omega+ (Existential)` (Seraph). Ghost-class combatants are the benchmark
all ladders measure against.
