# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 2026

## Overview

Boogie & The Yo-Yoz ("we," "our," "us," or "Company") operates the Boogie & The Yo-Yoz Merch mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your information when you use our Service.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, shipping address, phone number (for orders and support)
- **Payment Information:** Credit card and billing details are processed by Stripe and never stored on our servers
- **Communications:** Messages, feedback, and support requests you send us

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, app version, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Information:** Pages visited, features used, time spent in app, purchases made
- **Location Information:** General location based on IP address (not precise location)
- **Cookies & Tracking:** We use cookies and analytics to understand how you use the Service

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Process and fulfill orders
- Send order confirmations and shipping updates
- Provide customer support
- Improve the app and website
- Prevent fraud and enforce our terms
- Send promotional communications (with your consent)
- Comply with legal obligations

## Information Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal information. We may share information with:
- **Service Providers:** Stripe (payments), Shopify (product data), analytics platforms
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law or to protect our rights

## Data Retention

- **Order Data:** Retained for 7 years (legal/tax requirements)
- **Account Data:** Retained while your account is active; deleted upon request
- **Analytics:** Aggregated and anonymized data retained indefinitely

## Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) to protect your data. However, no method is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining your account password confidentiality.
